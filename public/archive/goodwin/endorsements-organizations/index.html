Should Vikki run for Lt. Governor in 2026?

Vikki has had the honor of representing District 47 in the Texas Legislature for four terms. She has always maintained that she might not be YOUR Representative, but she's done her best to represent YOU. With an eye toward the future of Texas, Vikki is now running for Lt. Governor. For too long, the current incumbent has been presiding over the deconstruction of Texas institutions - like Public Education and the Texas Senate.

It is time for a change!
